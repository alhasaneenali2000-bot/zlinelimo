About Zline

Started with one sedan and a pager.

Eleven years later, dispatch runs on flight-tracking software instead — but the standard hasn't moved: be there before the passenger is.

Why Austin needed this

Zline started in 2015 running one executive sedan between ABIA and downtown hotels, at a time when Austin's rideshare options couldn't reliably promise a fixed price or a scheduled pickup during SXSW week. The founding idea was simple: treat every ride like an airline treats a flight — tracked, timed, and accountable if it runs late.

That idea scaled with the city. As Austin grew — the tech corridor along MoPac, the Domain, COTA's arrival south of town — the fleet grew with it, but the dispatch philosophy stayed the same: fewer vehicles, better maintained, always tracked.
